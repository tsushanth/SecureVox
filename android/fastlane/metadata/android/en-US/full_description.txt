SecureVox records your voice and turns it into text on your phone. Transcription runs on the device with OpenAI's Whisper models. SecureVox does not upload your audio or transcripts. There is no account and no ads. Optional anonymous crash reports (app version, phone model, error type; never audio or text) are off unless you turn them on in Settings.

BEFORE YOU BUY: SETUP NEEDS INTERNET ONCE

The transcription model is not included in the app. The first time you open SecureVox it downloads the default model, Tiny (about 78 MB), from Hugging Face. You cannot record or transcribe until that download finishes. After that, recording and transcription work without a connection.

Better models are optional downloads inside the app: Base (about 148 MB) and Small (about 488 MB). Speaker labels, if you turn them on, need one more download of about 46 MB.

LANGUAGES: BE REALISTIC

The app offers 14 languages plus auto-detect. We measured accuracy on clean read speech, so noisy real-world recordings will do worse than these results.
- Good with the default Tiny model: English only.
- Good with Base (148 MB): English, Spanish, Korean.
- Good with Small (488 MB): English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Korean.
- Reduced accuracy even with Small: Dutch, Polish, Chinese (often written in Traditional characters), Arabic.
- Hindi is experimental. It is poor on every model, so treat the result as a rough draft at best.
If you mainly record in a language other than English, expect to download Small. It is slower and needs more memory; the app warns you if your phone is short on memory.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

- Record, with optional live captions while you speak (a rough preview; the saved transcript is made when you stop).
- Tap a word in the transcript to jump to that point in the audio.
- Optional speaker labels (Speaker 1, Speaker 2) for recordings up to 30 minutes. They are approximate.
- Import audio or video files, or share them into SecureVox from other apps.
- Export transcripts as TXT, SRT or VTT, or export the audio.
- Re-transcribe a recording with a different model.
- Custom dictionary for names and special terms.
- Favorites, recycle bin, optional app lock, Quick Settings tile, playback speed from 0.5x to 2x, light and dark themes.
- Read a transcript aloud with an offline voice installed on your phone.

WHAT SECUREVOX DOES NOT DO

- No summaries, AI notes or action items.
- No translation.
- No cloud sync and no account.
- No guarantee of accuracy: check anything important.

GOOD TO KNOW

- Needs Android 8.0 or newer and microphone permission.
- Larger models are slower and need more storage and memory.
- Long recordings are transcribed in the background with a notification and can take a while.
