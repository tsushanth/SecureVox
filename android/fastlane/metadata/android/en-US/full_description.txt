SecureVox records your voice and turns it into text on your phone. The transcription runs on-device with OpenAI's Whisper model, so your audio is never uploaded anywhere.

WHY SECUREVOX

Voice memos, meetings and interviews are often private. Most transcription apps send your audio to a server. SecureVox does not. There is no account, no cloud upload, no analytics and no tracking. The internet is only used once, to download the transcription model you choose.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Transcribe offline - Pick a Whisper model in the app: Tiny (about 78 MB) for speed, Base (about 148 MB) for a balance, or Small (about 488 MB) for the best accuracy. Models are downloaded inside the app.

• Live captions while you record - Watch the words appear as you speak. The full transcript is made when you stop.

• Speaker labels (optional) - Turn on Label speakers and the transcript is split into Speaker 1, Speaker 2 and so on. This needs a small extra download and works on recordings up to 30 minutes.

• Share or open audio and video from other apps - Send a voice message, a video or an audio file to SecureVox from any app and it is transcribed on your phone. You can also import files from inside the app.

• Quick start - Add the Quick Settings tile or use the launcher icon shortcut to start recording in one tap.

• Optional app lock - Protect the app with your fingerprint, face or screen lock.

• Export - Save or share transcripts as TXT, SRT or VTT subtitles, or share the audio itself.

• Custom dictionary - Add names and special terms so they are spelled the way you want.

• Re-transcribe - Run a recording again with a different model if you want better accuracy.

• Read aloud - Have a transcript read back to you with a voice that runs on your phone.

• Player with waveform - Drag the waveform to seek, change the speed from 0.5x to 2x, and search across all your transcripts.

• Light and dark themes with a calm, readable design.

LANGUAGES

SecureVox transcribes many languages. Quality varies by language: it is best in widely spoken languages such as English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Chinese and Japanese, and weaker in languages with less training data. Larger models help.

GOOD TO KNOW

• Accuracy depends on audio quality, accents and background noise. Check important transcripts before relying on them.
• Speaker labels are approximate and can mix up voices that sound alike.
• Larger models are slower and need more memory and storage.
• Requires Android 8.0 or newer.
