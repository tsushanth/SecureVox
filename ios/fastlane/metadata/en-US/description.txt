SecureVox - Your Private Voice Recording & Transcription App

Record voice memos and get accurate transcriptions without ever sending your data to the cloud. SecureVox uses on-device AI powered by the Whisper speech recognition model to transcribe your recordings completely offline.

Why SecureVox?

Your voice recordings contain sensitive information - meeting notes, personal thoughts, confidential conversations. Unlike other transcription apps that upload your audio to remote servers, SecureVox processes everything locally on your device. Your recordings never leave your phone.
