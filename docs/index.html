On-Device Transcription

Frequently Asked Questions

How does offline transcription work?

SecureVox uses OpenAI's Whisper model, which runs on your device (on iPhone it is optimized for Apple's Neural Engine). You download the speech model once, inside the app, from Hugging Face. After that, recording and transcription need no internet connection.

Which iPhones are supported?

SecureVox works on iPhone 12 and later with iOS 17+. Older iPhones can still use Apple's built-in speech recognition as a fallback.

How accurate is the transcription?

Accuracy depends on the model, the language, and the audio. Larger models are more accurate but slower, and clear audio helps. In our tests English is good with every model, while many other languages need the Small model, so check important transcripts before relying on them.

Is there a recording time limit?

SecureVox sets no time limit on recordings; they are limited by your free storage. Very long recordings take longer to transcribe.

Can I import existing audio files?

Yes! Import MP3, M4A, WAV, and other audio formats, or audio from video files. You can also share audio or video to SecureVox from other apps (Android).

Is my data backed up?

Your recordings are stored locally on your device. Device backups can include them: on iPhone, your iCloud backup (if enabled), and on Android, automatic backup to your Google account if you have backup turned on. SecureVox does not upload your audio or transcripts.
