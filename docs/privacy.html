Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

SecureVox is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how our voice recording and transcription app handles your data.

Data Collection and Storage

SecureVox is designed with privacy as a core principle:

SecureVox does not upload your audio or transcripts. Optional anonymous crash reports are off unless you turn them on in Settings.

  • Stored on your device. Your voice recordings, transcriptions, and settings are stored on your device.
  • No audio or transcript uploads. The app does not send your audio files or transcriptions to any server.
  • No account required. You don't need to create an account or provide any personal information to use the app.
  • On-device transcription. Transcription runs on your device using Whisper and other speech models. The app uses the internet to download the speech models you choose from public hosts (Hugging Face and GitHub); the downloads do not include your data.
  • Android backup. Like other Android apps, SecureVox's app data may be copied to your Google account by Android's automatic backup if you have device backup turned on. That is controlled by your device and Google account settings, not by SecureVox.

Optional Anonymous Crash Reports

Crash reports are optional and off by default. The app asks once, after your first finished transcription, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings under "Anonymous crash reports". Nothing is sent unless you turn them on.

If you turn them on, then when the app crashes or a feature such as recording, transcription, import or export fails, SecureVox sends a small report over an encrypted connection so we can fix the problem. A report contains only: the app version, the Android version, the phone model, the amount of free storage, the name of the feature that failed, and the type of error with the places in the app's code where it happened.

A report never contains your audio, transcripts, notes, recording titles, file names, file paths, email addresses or any other content of yours. It carries no account or device identifier, so we cannot tell which person it came from or delete one person's reports on request. Turning the setting off stops any further reports immediately.

Permissions

SecureVox requires the following permissions:

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Required to record voice memos. Audio is only recorded when you explicitly tap the record button, and recordings are stored locally on your device.
  • Internet: Used to download the speech models you choose, and to send optional anonymous crash reports if you turn them on. It is not used to upload your audio or transcripts.
  • Storage: SecureVox does not ask for a storage permission. Your recordings and transcriptions are saved in the app's own storage on your device.
  • Notifications: Used to show transcription progress when the app is in the background.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. We do not receive your recordings or transcriptions. The only information SecureVox can send to us is the optional anonymous crash report described above, and only if you turn it on. Your recordings and transcriptions leave your device only if you choose to export or share them using your device's share functionality, or through your device's own backup as described above.

Data Retention

Your recordings and transcriptions remain on your device until you choose to delete them. When you delete a recording within the app, it is removed from your device. Uninstalling the app removes its data from your device; any copy in your Google device backup is managed through your Google account.

Children's Privacy

SecureVox does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. The app does not collect personal information from any users; the optional crash reports contain none.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
